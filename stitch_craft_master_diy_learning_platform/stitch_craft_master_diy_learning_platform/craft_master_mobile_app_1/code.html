Earthy Handbuilt Pinch Pots

Discover the grounding sensation of molding natural stoneware clay using just your hands and simple wooden modeling tools.

photo_camera Crafter Community Gallery

"My very first pot!" - Liam
"Glazed with sage wash" - Maya
"Added organic rim" - Dan
"Perfect ring dish" - Sophie

build Tools & Supplies Needed

  • 500g Air-dry or stoneware clay
  • Wooden rib or sculpting spoon
  • Small bowl of clean water and sponge
Step 1 (20%) motion_photos_on

Forming the Clay Sphere

Roll your lump of clay firmly between both palms until you achieve an even, smooth sphere free of surface air pockets.

Step 2 (50%) pan_tool

Thumb Depression & Pinching

Gently press your right thumb straight down into the center, stopping about 1/2 inch from the base. Rotate and slowly pinch walls outward.

Step 3 (80%) brush

Smoothing the Rim & Base

Lightly moisten a natural sponge with water and glide it around the rim. Tap the base lightly onto the table to form a stable flat bottom.

lightbulb Crafter's Golden Tip

Work slowly and don't add too much water at once, or your clay walls will collapse from excessive moisture!
