## Brand & Style

The visual identity embodies tactile warmth, creative encouragement, and tranquil delight. Designed for crafters, hobbyists, and DIY creators of all experience levels, the aesthetic avoids corporate sleekness or utilitarian minimalism in favor of an artisanal, comforting atmosphere. It feels like stepping into a sunlit pottery studio filled with raw clay, pressed botanicals, linen, and woven yarn.

Key aesthetic characteristics:
- **Warm Handcrafted Touch:** Soft pillowy structures, organic rounded card geometries, and comforting contrast ratios.
- **Gentle Tactility:** Generous internal card padding, soft natural ambient drop shadows tinted with warm terracotta, and pill-shaped touch targets that feel cushiony and welcoming.
- **Playful Gamification:** Star-filled rewards trackers, rounded progress gauges, and warm ribbon-style badges celebrating daily creative streaks and milestones.
- **Form Factor:** App-centric mobile-first interface container centered within a grounding linen-like background canvas.

## Colors

The palette employs an earth-toned, pastel-glazed clay collection that remains soft without sacrificing contrast or legibility.

- **Background & Canvas (`#ECDAC9` Base Cream):** Serves as the overarching warm foundation, eliminating harsh digital whites and giving the app a tactile paper-linen glow.
- **Surface Elevation (`#F6EDE5` & `#E8CDB8` Soft Peach):** Card bodies rest on soft cream surfaces, highlighted with soft peach for elevated modules, nested lists, and highlighted card segments.
- **Accents & Subtle Framing (`#C8CCBD` Spring Sage):** Used for delicate 1px border outlines, dividers, inactive states, and soothing decorative foliage accents.
- **Secondary Containers & Badges (`#D0AA86` Beach Sand):** Provides grounded golden-warm warmth for category tags, course chapter progress meters, and reward ribbon backdrops.
- **Text Accents & Secondary Controls (`#858E79` Reseda Green):** Provides an earthy botanical balance to terracotta. Used for secondary buttons, completion indicators, and subtle meta-labels.
- **Primary CTAs & Active States (`#BE816E` Red Brick):** A rich, fired-terracotta tint dedicated to primary interactive triggers, active tab indicators, enrollment buttons, and bright celebration accents.
- **Text Hierarchy (`#3B332C` Cocoa Bark & `#6E6258` Warm Ochre):** Warm espresso dark tones replacing pure black to maintain optimal text readability without breaking the cozy mood.

## Typography

Quicksand drives the typography system. Its open counters, rounded terminal points, and organic rhythm provide an unpretentious, friendly demeanor that welcomes learners.

- **Headlines:** Set in `font-bold` (700) or `font-semibold` (600) with slight negative letter spacing on larger sizes to maintain cohesion and prevent wide tracking.
- **Body Copy:** Set in `font-medium` (500) to ensure legibility across soft tinted backdrops. Avoid ultra-thin or light font weights.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Rendered with elevated weights (700) and slight positive letter-spacing to enhance legibility on badges, buttons, and milestone metrics.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a mobile-first philosophy, constrained within a centered viewport container (`max-w-[430px]`) on desktop viewports with a subtle framed drop-shadow backdrop mimicking a personal creative journal or dedicated pocket studio app.

- **Mobile Viewport:** Margin padding is locked to `16px` (`1rem`) to maximize card real estate while leaving breathing room.
- **Vertical Flow:** Standard component gap is `16px` (`1rem`), expanding to `24px` (`1.5rem`) between unrelated course sections or dashboard categories.
- **Grid Structure:** Inside course views, a 2-column equal-width grid handles 50/50 split square cards with `12px` or `16px` gutters.
- **Rewards Module:** Positioned either as a sticky header badge or an expansive top card spanning full container width with embedded star tracks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, diffuse ambient layering rather than harsh black drop shadows.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Base: `#ECDAC9` (Base Canvas)
  - Secondary Container: `#E8CDB8` (Sections, inactive trays, header wells)
  - Card Surfaces: `#F6EDE5` (Elevated course tiles, modals, popovers)
- **Ambient Shadows:** All shadows utilize warm brown-brick undertones.
  - *Soft Rest:* `0px 4px 14px -2px rgba(110, 80, 60, 0.08)`
  - *Pill Float / Active:* `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(190, 129, 110, 0.22)`
- **Border Integration:** Low-contrast borders (`1.5px solid #C8CCBD` or `1px solid rgba(133, 142, 121, 0.2)`) define edges against lighter backdrops, giving surfaces the quality of cut clay or hand-stitched linen.

## Shapes

The interface embraces oversized rounded corners to maintain a plush, friendly atmosphere.

- **Cards & Outer Shells:** Rounded to `24px` (`rounded-2xl`) or `32px` (`rounded-3xl`). Inner corners maintain nested concentric curvature (`16px` to `20px`).
- **Interactive Controls:** All buttons, filter chips, pill badges, and input containers use fully rounded pill geometry (`rounded-full` / `9999px`).
- **Media Containers:** Image banners and craft project thumbnails feature `20px` corner radii with subtle inset borders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#BE816E` (Red Brick) with `#FFFFFF` text. Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `14px 24px`, font-weight 700. Features a warm terracotta drop shadow `0 6px 18px rgba(190,129,110,0.28)`.
- **Secondary CTA:** Background `#858E79` (Reseda Green) with `#FFFFFF` text or `#F6EDE5` surface with `#858E79` border (`1.5px`) and text.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Background transparent, text `#3B332C`, soft hover tint `#E8CDB8`.

### Fifty-Fifty Split Square Course Cards
- **Structure:** Square aspect-ratio (1:1) modules arranged in a 2-column grid.
- **Top Half (50%):** Image mask of craft project (pottery, knitting, watercolor) with a rounded inner frame (`rounded-2xl`) and an organic tag overlay (e.g., "Beginner" on `#C8CCBD`).
- **Bottom Half (50%):** Background `#F6EDE5` or `#E8CDB8`. Features a compact course title in 2 lines (`headline-sm`), author stamp, duration pill, and progress bar.
- **Border:** 1px soft border in `#C8CCBD`.

### Cute Rewards Bar & Star Track
- **Bar Shell:** Elevated pill-card container filled with `#F6EDE5` and bounded by a `1.5px` `#D0AA86` border.
- **Star Counter:** Golden terracotta star icons (`#BE816E` or `#D0AA86`) with glowing ambient halo. Displayed alongside a playful progress meter.
- **Progress Track:** Track trough in `#ECDAC9`, filled with an animated gradient from `#D0AA86` to `#BE816E`, capped with an illustrated star pin.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Active State:** `#BE816E` background, `#FFFFFF` text, subtle shadow.
- **Inactive State:** `#F6EDE5` background, `#6E6258` text, border `1px solid #C8CCBD`.
- **Height:** 36px with horizontal padding of 16px.

### Inputs & Text Fields
- **Container:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) or soft cushion (`rounded-2xl`), background `#F6EDE5`, border `1.5px solid #C8CCBD`.
- **Text & Placeholder:** Input text `#3B332C`, placeholder `#6E6258` with 60% opacity.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#BE816E` with an ambient glow ring of `0 0 0 3px rgba(190,129,110,0.2)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Shape:** Checkboxes use `rounded-lg` (8px), Radios use circular `rounded-full`.
- **Selected State:** Filled with `#BE816E`, displaying a clean cream checkmark or inner pip (`#ECDAC9`).
- **Unselected State:** Background `#F6EDE5`, border `2px solid #C8CCBD`.
